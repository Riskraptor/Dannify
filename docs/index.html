    Questions

    Is Dannify free?

    Yes. Free to download and use, with no ads.

    Does it update itself?

    Yes. You install Dannify once; after that, every copy keeps itself up to date.

    Is it safe?

    Every file is signed by Dannify, and the app checks the signature of each update before installing it.

    Windows says it protected my PC

    Choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows asks only the first time.

    My Mac says Dannify cannot be opened

    Right-click Dannify in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. On macOS 15 and later: open Dannify and click Done, then in System Settings, Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway.

    My phone says Play Protect does not know the app

    Tap More details, then Install anyway.

    Will it run on my TV stick?

    If it is 64-bit, yes. Many older sticks are 32-bit and cannot run Dannify yet.

    Is there Dannify for iPhone or iPad?

    Not yet, sorry. For now, you can enjoy Dannify on your computer, your Android phone or tablet, and your TV.
