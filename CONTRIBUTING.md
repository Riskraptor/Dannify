# Helping Dannify grow

Dannify is made by one person, and it gets better fastest with the help of the
people who use it. Here is how to help. Thank you for every bit of it.

## Tell me what went wrong

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Riskraptor/Dannify/issues/new/choose) and
pick **Something went wrong**. Say what you did, what you expected, and what
happened instead, and which version of Dannify you have (Settings → About); a
screenshot helps a lot.

## Suggest an idea

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Riskraptor/Dannify/issues/new/choose) and
pick **An idea**, or start a conversation in
[Discussions](https://github.com/Riskraptor/Dannify/discussions). Say what you
would like to do, and why: the "why" is what makes an idea easy to build well.

## Put Dannify in your language

Dannify speaks English, Français, Español, Português, Deutsch and Kiswahili.
To add your language, or to fix a word in one of them, open an issue and pick
**A translation**. A translation that reads naturally to someone who speaks
the language is worth more than a word-for-word one.

## Share lyrics

Every song's lyrics in Dannify are shared with everyone else who plays it.
When a song has none, or they are out of time, press **Add lyrics** or
**Improve** in the lyrics panel: type the words, tap along to time them, and
the next person to play the song gets them in sync.

## Changes to the code

Pull requests are welcome for fixes and small improvements. For anything
bigger, open an issue first so we can agree on it before you spend time on it.

- Keep a change to one thing, and say in the description what it fixes or
  adds and how you checked it.
- `npx vitest run` (in `frontend`) and `pytest` (in `Backend`) should pass.
- Every text on screen goes through the translations in
  `frontend/src/i18n/locales`, in every language.

By sending a contribution you agree to the terms in the
[Contributions](LICENSE) part of the licence: you keep the copyright in what
you wrote, and Dannify may use it.

## What is not allowed

Dannify is not open source. Its [licence](LICENSE) lets anyone use it and read
its code, and asks that nobody copies it, changes it outside this repository,
or publishes it under another name. If you want to build on it, open an issue
and ask: I would rather hear your idea than see a copy.
