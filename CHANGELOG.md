# Changelog

What changed in each release of Dannify. The newest is at the top. Every
release, with its downloads, is on the [releases
page](https://github.com/Riskraptor/Dannify/releases).

## 4.18.0

Rooms that feel alive, updates that look after themselves, and a Dannify
that pauses and comes back gracefully.

- **Rooms.** The code boxes take every click and every paste, a bare code,
  an invite link or a whole message with a code in it, on a laptop as on a
  phone. You see who is typing; messages are grouped, can be reacted to and
  can mention someone with @, and a mention or a message while Dannify is
  out of sight comes as a notification, with a soft sound if you like. A
  code lets new people in for 12 hours, and the host can make a new one at
  once, which ends the old one.
- **Updates.** A new update page: the new version, its size, what it
  brings, and a download you can watch, with the time left. On Android,
  setup offers to allow updates in one tap, Dannify finds new versions in
  the background, says so once, and after an update a tap opens it again.
  What is new in a version is shown once, with a short tour of it.
- **When Dannify is paused.** The pause covers everything, setup included,
  and keeps the window's own buttons, so it can still be moved, minimised
  and closed. It counts down to when Dannify is back, lifts by itself on
  time, and everything comes back as it was, setup and all. On Android the
  notification and the widgets say so too. When it lifts, a welcome back.
- **Faster.** A pause, a notice or a fix reaches every open copy within a
  minute, not twenty.
- **Desktop.** Notifications of Dannify's own, crisp icons at every size,
  shortcuts that show the new icon after an update, and a window that no
  longer grows each time it is minimised.

## 4.17.0

Listening together grows a chat, lyrics get more accurate, and everything
you play is the same on every device.

- **Talk in a room.** Listen together has a chat: messages reach whoever is
  in the room at that moment and are kept nowhere, gone when you leave. New
  ones float up over the app; the host can turn the chat off. The room's
  window has tabs for the room, its chat and its people, and guests see
  what is playing and the next five songs.
- **Your name, and invites that open the room.** The name offered is your
  device's own (your phone's name, or the person signed in to a computer),
  kept once you change it, and changeable in the room. The code goes into
  six boxes, typed or pasted, a whole invite too; an invite carries a link
  that opens the room in Dannify, and gets Dannify for whoever does not
  have it.
- **More accurate lyrics.** The most accurate timed lyrics come first,
  unless their timing runs past the end of the song; timed lyrics always
  beat words alone. The lyrics search shows one list of only this song,
  the surest first: its artist, about its length, no live take or remix of
  an original. Lyrics published from the editor show in the player at once.
- **The lyrics editor follows the song** wherever it is moved from (the
  player's bar, Now Playing, a headset), and a move out of a looped line
  ends the loop. Refreshing lyrics no longer blinks the words away.
- **Your listening, everywhere.** Signed in, what you play on every device
  is one history, one set of stats and one Soundtrack, kept up to date by
  itself, with nothing to turn on.
- **Phones.** Standard player controls (a round play button and plain
  icons), every page of Your Soundtrack fits the screen, and a page opened
  from a shortcut sits under its own place on the bottom bar.
- **The tour** shows the whole of Dannify: pasting links, the mixes,
  lyrics, listening together, Your Soundtrack, Dannify on a phone and TV,
  and the keyboard.
- **Dannify everywhere.** What the app says no longer names other services,
  except where you sign in.

## 4.16.0

Dannify on Android, phones and tablets, and a calmer, clearer app
everywhere.

- **Dannify for Android.** The whole app on your phone: the same library,
  search, lyrics, stats and Your Soundtrack, with the backend running on
  the phone itself. Made for a phone, not squeezed from a computer: a
  bottom bar, a mini player in the cover's colour, Now Playing on one
  screen with the lyrics and the queue a tap away, and menus and questions
  that come up from the bottom with big buttons.
- **Listen together.** Start a room, share its six-letter code, and
  everyone in it hears the same song at the same moment on their own
  device, wherever they are: each device keeps itself in step with the
  host, by a hair faster or slower or a jump when it has to. Guests suggest
  songs from any song's menu, send reactions that float up every screen,
  and can pause just for themselves and catch up. The host lets people in
  or not, hands the room on, and ends it. Free for everyone, no account
  needed, on phones, computers and TVs alike.
- **On the home screen and the lock screen.** A player widget in three
  sizes that takes the cover's colour, a media notification with like,
  shuffle and repeat, shortcuts on the app's icon, and sharing Your
  Soundtrack to any app.
- **Tablets.** Held upright, the phone's layout; turned, the full layout,
  without the computer's volume slider or window arrows.
- **On the television too.** Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV run the same
  app, listed in the TV's launcher and moved with the remote: the arrows go
  where you would expect, OK plays, Back goes back, and what is chosen is
  always plain to see from the sofa.
- **Updates on a phone.** A notification when a new version is ready (the
  only one Dannify sends of its own); downloads show their progress in the
  app only. The welcome asks for it once, and About has a switch for it.
- **Downloads in one place.** The songs on your device are "Downloaded",
  and the songs on their way sit at the top of it while they come in, each
  with its progress, a stop button, and Retry failed and Stop all for the
  lot. "Remove download" takes a song off the device.
- **Lyrics when you ask for them.** The lyrics panel no longer opens by
  itself when a song starts; it is in Settings if you want it back.
- **Writing lyrics on a phone.** Adding or fixing a song's lyrics is three
  pages of the app: write the words (with Paste and Repeat lines at your
  thumb), tap along as each line starts, then hear it back before you
  publish. The puzzle LRCLIB asks for before it takes them is solved on
  every core of the phone, several times faster than before. A TV shows
  lyrics and leaves writing them to a phone or a computer.
- **Lyrics from YouTube Music too.** Timed lyrics now come from YouTube
  Music itself (licensed from Musixmatch or LyricFind) as well as LRCLIB,
  and the closer match is shown, saying where it came from. Songs that had
  no lyrics before now have them.
- **Search for lyrics yourself.** When the lyrics are wrong or missing,
  search both places by title and artist: each result says whether it is
  timed, how its length compares with the song's, and shows its first
  lines. Pick one and it stays with the song; go back to the automatic
  choice any time.
- **Lyrics a little early or late?** Nudge them by a tenth or a whole
  second, and hold the button to keep going. In the editor, tap a line's
  time to type it, nudge it, take it from where the song is, or hear the
  song from there, and move every line after it along with it.
- **Applause when they go out.** Publishing lyrics ends with confetti and
  clapping, thanks for the song, and then the song itself, with its new
  words lit as it plays.
- **Your Soundtrack, found.** When it has something worth seeing (your
  first story, a week or month just gone, a milestone in hours, a new top
  artist), a card at the top of Home says so, once, and stays quiet if you
  put it away.
- **Your listening in tabs on a phone**: Overview, Songs, Artists and When,
  instead of one long page.
- **Report a problem, one question at a time** on a phone.
- **Signing in with Google on a phone** opens in a proper frame: close,
  where the page is from, reload, and a clear word when you are offline.
- **Artists and albums, the way you know them.** On a phone, an artist's
  page opens on their photo across the screen, with their monthly
  listeners, their five most popular songs, albums and singles in rows that
  slide, "Fans also like" and an About card. An album ends with "More by"
  its artist, everywhere. The top bar takes the colour of the picture, and
  a play button rides it once you scroll.
- **The queue says what comes next**: where the songs are from, and, when
  nothing is queued, that songs like the last one will follow.
- **Back, the way a phone's apps go back.** Each place on the bottom bar
  keeps its own pages, and back steps through the one you are in, then to
  Home as you left it, then out, with the music playing on. Switching
  between places is no longer a trail back has to walk again.
- **Settings on a phone, laid out like Spotify's**: search in the top bar,
  the theme and your listening history a tap away at the top, each section
  saying what is in it, and only what a phone has.
- **Dannify on your phone, from your computer.** About and the side bar
  show a QR code: scan it with your phone's camera and it opens the newest
  version for Android.
- **Next, next, next.** Press Next or Back as fast as you like: Dannify
  waits until you stop, then fetches only that song, so the music starts
  sooner. The play button says when a song is on its way.
- **Shared links show the songs**, not how Dannify finds them.
- **A first start that sits right**: the welcome comes straight after the
  start screen, clear of a phone's status bar, its buttons where a thumb
  reaches, and no keyboard popping up by itself.
- **The equalizer, by finger.** Its curve shows the setting even with
  nothing playing; on a touch screen a dot moves by how far your finger
  goes, and a double-tap puts it back to 0.
- **When something serious goes wrong**, Dannify can be paused for
  everyone for a short while, or a part of it switched off, and comes back
  as it was, nothing lost. The screen says why in a line, counts down when
  it knows when, shows whether you are online and when it last looked, and
  offers the fix the moment there is one. The "update required" screen got
  the same care.
- **A new icon**: a D folded from a green ribbon, a play button in its
  hollow and sound on either side, on a dark glass tile with a glowing rim,
  on every platform.
- **Calmer words for updates**: no more "Ta-da!".

Plus fixes: a tap on a song in a list plays it on a phone instead of
opening its artist, pages on a phone no longer lose a strip down their right edge
to a computer's scrollbar, Settings' search on a phone keeps its field
while you type, a video's date or view count is never taken for an artist (one
topped Your Soundtrack as "Apr 16, 2025"), streams on a slow line get time
to connect and a second try, and a version can be required, so an old copy
updates before anything else.

## 4.15.0

Podcasts and Spotify links, a guided tour, special days with pages of their
own, the charts where you are, richer stats, and an app at ease offline.

- **A guided tour.** After setup, Dannify walks you round itself: a
  spotlight on one thing at a time, an arrow to it, and a few steps you try
  yourself. After a big update, a short tour of what is new. Both are in
  Settings, General and the command palette, any time.
- **Paste a Spotify link, anywhere.** A song, album, playlist or artist
  opens on a page of its own, every song ready to play, save, or keep as a
  playlist of yours in one click. YouTube and YouTube Music links open too,
  channels by their @name included.
- **Podcasts.** A Spotify podcast or episode link opens the same show on
  YouTube Music, every episode ready to play. Long plays get steps of 15 and
  30 seconds and the listening speed right in the player.
- **Special days, a page each.** Each day opens in its own colours and
  weather, with a countdown ticking to it, its playlists and songs, what you
  played on it last year, and the days after it.
- **Right About Now.** A new mix of what you tend to play at this hour, on
  this kind of day. It changes with the hour.
- **Charts and new releases.** The charts and top artists where you are, on
  Home and Search, and what is newly out.
- **Your stats, deeper.** Highlights (your biggest day, longest sitting, an
  average day, the song on repeat), your week day by day, the songs new to
  you, and when your YouTube Music listening last came in.
- **Now Playing, easy to find.** The cover flies into the big player and
  back; a hint shows the way once; Ctrl+E opens it from anywhere.
- **At ease offline.** A calm "Offline" sign, your saved music a click away
  on any page that needs the internet, and pages that come back by
  themselves.
- **Write lyrics as you listen.** The song plays beside the words; adding
  lyrics never changes the song's details.
- **Fixes on the spot.** Important fixes, like a change in how YouTube
  answers, reach you without waiting for a release, and an urgent update
  says so and asks before it installs. A fix build reads "4.15.0 · fix 1".
- **Report a problem, your way.** Choose what goes with it; see exactly what
  would be sent. The activity log is sealed: only the developer can read it.
- **Closing the window quits**, unless you turn on "Keep playing when I
  close the window" in Settings, General.
- **Support is GitHub Sponsors only.** M-Pesa, PayPal and Ko-fi are gone.

Plus fixes: checking for updates works on busy networks (it used to be
refused when a whole network shared GitHub's 60 checks an hour, and then
said "up to date" anyway), the window keeps its size through maximize and
restore, every
Browse category opens again, a restart that cannot replace the old copy
stops cleanly, and the developer-style buttons (refresh pictures, refresh
recommendations, copy link) are gone because it all keeps itself fresh.

## 4.13.0

Every special day of your year, your listening from everywhere, your
language, and lyrics done right.

- **Special days, all year, wherever you are.** Dannify knows the days your
  part of the world celebrates and celebrates them with you: the New Year,
  Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Eid, Holi, Easter, Vesak, Diwali, Hanukkah,
  Christmas and many more, each on your country's own date, plus your
  country's own days in its flag's colours (in Kenya: Madaraka, Mashujaa
  and Jamhuri Days). Every day moves its own way: snow sways down, lanterns
  swing, Diwali's lamps flicker, bats flit across on Halloween. Each has a
  greeting and a "Music for…" shelf on Home. It is always on, works
  offline, and asks no one where you are; Settings, Appearance shows your
  region and the special days ahead.
- **Your YouTube Music listening, in Your stats.** Signed in with Google,
  what you play on YouTube Music on your phone, the web or a speaker counts
  in History, Your stats and Your year in music, so a copy installed today
  starts with all of it, and keeps up by itself without counting a song
  twice. It can be turned off in Settings, Accounts.
- **Six languages, and yours from the first start.** Kiswahili, Español,
  Português and Deutsch join English and Français. Dannify starts in your
  computer's language and follows it; pick another in Settings, Appearance,
  or on the welcome screen. Switching cross-fades smoothly, and the startup
  screen speaks your language too.
- **Lyrics in time, made easy.** Lyrics early or late? Open Timing and
  click the line you are hearing: the words line up to it, and it is kept
  for the song. Several versions? Pick from a list that says how each is
  timed and how long it is, with the best match marked. Dannify also finds
  the right lyrics more often, telling a remix or a live take from the song.
- **Writing lyrics, faster.** Type a chorus once and repeat it (Ctrl+D)
  wherever it comes back. While timing, the line being sung is marked, so
  the line to tap next is plainly the next one.
- **The player, clearer.** Save shows a ring filling as the song saves, so
  there is no pressing it twice. ⋯ holds everything about the song playing.
  On Now Playing, the Lyrics and Queue buttons switch what is beside the
  artwork. The sound panel is tidier: the equalizer's sounds in one row,
  one set of speeds, and a balance you can read.
- **Downloads, without the worry.** The Downloads page says plainly that it
  is only a list: clearing it never deletes a song.
- **Faster and smoother.** Dannify reads less at every start, and every
  change of look, language or page was measured and stays well under a
  frame's notice.
- **Polished all over.** Every page, panel and dialog was gone over in
  light and dark, large and small, in every language: long names wrap
  instead of being cut, tabs fit their words, pages say what they are the
  same way, and palettes line up in even rows.

Fixed:

- Songs a radio or "Keep the music playing" added to the queue came without
  their pictures and lengths; only the song about to play got them. Every
  song in "Next up" has them now.
- Sharing lyrics could fail with a message from the lyrics service itself;
  a passing fault is now tried again for you, and anything else is said
  plainly in your language.
- The Crossfade setting in the sound panel read "{count} s".
- Leaving the mini player put the window back a pixel smaller each time.
- A part of Dannify fetched when first opened (like the mini player) could
  stay blank if its files were replaced while Dannify was open.
- Two lengths of one song could share each other's lyrics.
- Home asked for the same listening summary twice.

From this version Dannify has a licence of its own: free to download and
use, and not to be copied. See the licence on the project's page.

## 4.11.0

Music made for you.

- **Made for you.** Home has mixes that make themselves and keep up with
  your listening: On Repeat (what you keep coming back to), Top This Month,
  Forgotten Favourites (songs you loved and have not played in a while) and
  Recently Added. Each one plays, shuffles and saves as a playlist, and its
  picture is made from the covers of what is in it.
- **Your year in music.** Your minutes, top artist, top songs and albums,
  favourite time to listen, longest run of days and the artists you
  discovered, told as a story you step through. It opens from Home and from
  Stats, and earlier years are a click away.
- **Word-by-word lyrics.** The line being sung fills in word by word. Lyrics
  that come with the time of every word are followed exactly; the rest are
  paced by syllable, a little behind the voice rather than ahead of it. It
  can be turned off in Settings, Lyrics.
- **A living Now Playing.** The background drifts slowly in the colours of
  the artwork while the music plays, and settles when it stops. It stays
  still when the system asks for reduced motion.
- **The music keeps going offline.** When the queue runs out and YouTube
  Music's mix cannot be had, Dannify carries on from your library: more by
  the same artist first, then the same album, then the rest.
- **Show on Discord.** Your Discord profile can show the song you are
  playing, "Listening to Dannify", with its artwork, a progress bar that
  follows pauses and skips, and a button for friends to play it on YouTube
  Music. It talks to the Discord app on your computer directly, connects by
  itself whenever Discord is open, and is off until you turn it on in
  Settings, Playback.

Fixed:

- Lyrics with word timings no longer show the timing marks (`<00:12.34>`)
  among the words.
- Arrow keys in Your year in music step through it instead of seeking the
  song.

## 4.10.0

The speed release.

- **Your library opens at once, however big it is.** What each saved song is
  gets remembered between runs, so only songs that changed are read again: a
  library of 5,000 songs is ready in about half a second instead of about
  nine. Questions asked together (songs, artists, albums) look at the folder
  once.
- **Smoother, lighter lists.** A saved song's artwork is drawn from a small
  copy, made once and kept, instead of the full picture every time: long
  lists scroll smoothly, use far less memory, and after the first time ask
  the app for no artwork at all.
- **Next is instant.** A few seconds into a song, the next one is already
  loaded, artwork included, so it starts the moment Next is pressed.
- **A lighter start.** The mini player, the dialogs, the lyrics editor and
  other languages load when they are first needed, and the download engine
  when the first download needs it. The interface's main script is 17%
  smaller, with a size budget in CI so it stays that way.

## 4.9.0

### New

- **Albums in your library.** Your Library has an Albums page: every album you
  have saved songs from, sorted by when you added it, by name or by artist.
  Each album opens to its songs in album order, numbered by their real track
  numbers, with Play, Shuffle, the song menus, and **Full album**, which opens
  the whole album on YouTube Music to save the rest of it. "Go to album" on a
  saved song opens the album in your library.
- **Listening history and stats.** Dannify keeps count of what you listen to
  (a listen is 30 seconds, or half of a short song). **History** lists it day
  by day, and **Your stats** shows time listened, streaks, the hours you
  listen at, and your top songs, artists and albums for the last week, month,
  year or all time. Home gains *On repeat* and *Rediscover*. The history stays
  on your computer; it can be paused or cleared in Settings, and one listen can
  be taken out of it.
- **ListenBrainz.** Connect your ListenBrainz account in Settings with your user
  token, and what you listen to goes to your profile as you play it. Listens
  played offline are sent when you are back online. Nothing is sent unless you
  connect an account.
- **Command palette.** Ctrl+Shift+P (Cmd+Shift+P on a Mac) finds any page,
  playback control, setting, playlist, artist or album by typing a few letters.
- **Search suggestions** as you type in the search box.
- **Shortcuts:** L likes the song that is playing, Ctrl+D saves it, and 0 to 9
  jump through it.

### Repair and recovery

- When saved songs cannot be opened on this account (a new PC, a new Windows
  account, a profile restored from a backup), Dannify used to stop saving
  anything and suggest a restart that changed nothing. The message now has two
  ways out: **Try again**, for a keyring that was still locked when Dannify
  started, and **Start fresh**, which lets new songs save again without
  deleting anything, after which the old songs can be repaired.
- A music folder that has gone (an unplugged drive) can be chosen again, or
  checked again, from the message itself.
- **Repair all** says so when nothing needs repairing, instead of doing nothing.
- A repaired song keeps its album, and every original a repair replaces is
  kept for two weeks, not only the latest one.
- What a crash or a power cut left behind in the middle of a repair is tidied
  up the next time Dannify starts.

### Fixed

- Saving a single song lost its album, so songs saved one at a time sat loose
  under "Singles" on artist pages. They keep their album now, and **Look up
  missing albums** on the Albums page finds the album for songs saved before.
- A download whose stream YouTube refused part-way (HTTP 403) failed; it now
  retries the slower, sure way, as playback already did.
- Liked Songs said you had liked nothing when it could not load. It says it
  could not load, and loads by itself once you are back online.
- The support note counted every song that started, skips included; it counts
  real listens now.
- A release that does not yet have the files for your system is no longer
  offered as an update that can only fail to download.

### Under the hood

- Libraries with published security advisories were updated: starlette 1.3.1,
  urllib3 2.8.0 and anyio 4.14.2. Also ytmusicapi 1.12.3, certifi 2026.7.22,
  Vue 3.5.44 and axios 1.20.0.
